A record of the shower choices you already make, with room for rest. Keep your usual shower, use your existing timer, rest, or skip. Cold is entirely optional: this sheet is not a cold-exposure program, and there is no target to beat or requirement to fill every date.

Use this log on paper or copy it into your own notes; this page has no record-entry or upload fields. No phone, app, or signup is needed. To print a blank copy, use your browser's Print command and choose a printer or Save as PDF.

Your choice comes before the record

Cold exposure is a physiological stressor. Stop if you feel unwell, and ask a qualified healthcare professional first if you have any concern about suitability. Do not do breathing exercises or hold your breath in water. This log is not a medical tool or medical advice, and it is never a reason to prolong a session.
