Choose one small thing you both want to do tonight, with room to change the plan. Use the short process below, then fill in the blank card on paper or in your own notes. No app, account, signup, or download is needed.

Keep the plan in your own hands: This page has no record-entry or upload fields. Print it or copy the prompts into your own notes, and choose where to keep them. To make a paper copy, use your browser's Print command; choose Save as PDF there if your browser offers it.

Make a choice that fits tonight

  1. Start with the real limits

    Agree how much time you have, including travel and getting home, and a total budget that feels comfortable to both of you. Zero spending is an option. Decide whether to stay in or head out, and mention any access, transport, food, noise, or other preferences that affect the choice.

    Each person can name one thing they are up for and one thing they want to avoid. Neither person needs to explain or negotiate away a boundary.

  2. Pick one activity and an easy backup

    Choose one small activity you both want, rather than filling the whole evening. If neither of you wants the available options, choose another time or leave tonight unplanned. Agree a simpler backup for changes in weather, timing, or energy; it is also fine to stop or cancel.

  3. Make the invitation specific

    Put the activity, place, and start time into a short invitation, with the finish time or time available and backup if useful. Check that the other person wants the plan before treating it as agreed. Share the wording yourself in whatever way you normally use.

Two original web examples

These ideas were written for this page. They are not presented as entries from Dahlia's in-app decks; adapt them or choose something else.

  • A small playlist swap: At home, each choose a song and, if you want, tell the story behind it. Agree a comfortable volume and finish time. A simple backup is to talk about the songs without playing them.
  • A short outing with a stay-home option: Choose a nearby place and a route that work for both people's access needs, transport, time, and budget. Check opening hours and access information yourself before heading out. If weather or energy changes, stay home and each share a photo of a place you like instead.

Your blank choose-tonight card

Write a few words under each heading on the printed page or copy the card into your own notes. Keep only the details you need.

Time available

Start, finish, and any travel time to allow for.

Agreed budget

A comfortable total, including any travel or food; zero is fine.

Place and preferences

Staying in or heading out; location, access needs, transport, and anything else that would help it fit.

What would fit, and what to avoid

Room for each person's preferences and boundaries.

Chosen activity

Easy backup

Invitation wording

Include what, where, and when. Leave room for the other person to suggest a change or decline.

A plan, not a promise

This is a paper planning aid, not therapy or matchmaking, and it does not promise a relationship outcome. It does not book activities, pair or sync accounts, or send invitations. Check any real-world details yourself, share your own invitation, and change or leave the plan whenever either person wants to.
