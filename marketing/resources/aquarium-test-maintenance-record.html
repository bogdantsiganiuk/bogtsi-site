Keep water-test readings and maintenance notes together in a blank record you can reuse. Print this page or copy the labels into your own notes. No app, sensor, account, or signup is required.

This page has no record-entry or upload fields. To use it on paper, choose Print in your browser; you can also choose Save as PDF where your browser offers it.

Record what you measured

Use one water-test block for each parameter you measured. Keep the measured value with its unit and test kit or method so the reading retains its context. Copy or print another block whenever you need more room.

This is a record sheet, not a care or dosing plan. It does not set target ranges, interpret readings, recommend stocking or animal-health actions, or choose a maintenance schedule.
