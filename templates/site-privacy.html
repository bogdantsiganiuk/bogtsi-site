Website privacy.

Updated November 18, 2026. This page covers bogtsi.xyz itself. Each app has its own privacy policy, available in the app support directory. The older /privacy address still redirects to Halcyon’s app policy; it is not this website’s policy.

Aggregate website analytics.

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate page views, visits, page performance, referring sites, and device, browser, and country summaries. These are approximate measurements, not an exact count of unique people. The analytics beacon does not use cookies, browser storage, or fingerprinting to identify visitors across visits. We do not add custom events, session replay, advertising, or an account system.

The Cloudflare script is manually embedded on this website’s homepage, eleven app product pages, seven free tool and guide pages, the resource directory, the support directory, and this website privacy page. It is not embedded in the sixty app-scoped legal/support pages, the legacy app-policy redirects, or the instant forwarding pages for retired resource addresses. Loading an included page makes ordinary network requests to Cloudflare for the script and analytics. See Cloudflare Web Analytics documentation, its measurement and privacy FAQ, and Cloudflare’s privacy policy for the provider’s practices.

Your content stays yours.

Some free tools on this website have buttons and number or selection fields. They run entirely in your browser: nothing you enter is sent or stored, and the site never requests microphone access. The website has no fields for medical information, reflections, recordings, or other personal-content uploads. This does not prevent ordinary hosting and analytics network requests when you load a page.

Hosting still involves a connection.

Your browser sends ordinary network requests to load these pages and images. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address and request details to serve and secure its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices. Cookie-free analytics does not mean no network traffic or no hosting logs.

Links and email.

App Store links take you to Apple. Apple’s own terms and privacy practices apply there; purchases happen through Apple, not this website. If you choose to email support, your email provider and ours process that message. We use the information you send to respond to your request. Please do not include sensitive personal content.

Search visibility.

The site publishes a sitemap and allows search engines to index its public pages. Search Console verification, if separately authorized by the owner, is separate from the Cloudflare analytics described above.
