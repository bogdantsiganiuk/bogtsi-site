Website privacy.

Updated November 15, 2026. This page covers bogtsi.xyz itself. Each app has its own privacy policy, available in the app support directory. The older /privacy address still redirects to Halcyon’s app policy; it is not this website’s policy.

A static site, without visitor tracking.

This site has no analytics SDK, tracking pixel, advertising, cookies, session replay, or account system. It does not request microphone access, collect reflections, or offer a form for uploading recordings or other personal content. Resource worksheets can be read or printed without sending your answers anywhere.

Hosting still involves a connection.

Your browser sends ordinary network requests to load these pages and images. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address and request details to serve and secure its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices. “No tracking” does not mean no network traffic or no hosting logs.

Links and email.

App Store links take you to Apple. Apple’s own terms and privacy practices apply there; purchases happen through Apple, not this website. If you choose to email support, your email provider and ours process that message. We use the information you send to respond to your request. Please do not include sensitive personal content.

Search visibility.

The site publishes a sitemap and allows search engines to index its public pages. Search Console verification, if separately authorized by the owner, does not add visitor tracking code to this site.

Questions.

Contact support@bogtsi.xyz about this website or its privacy practices.
